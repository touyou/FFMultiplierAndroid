Times tables, all the way to F.

FFMultiplier is a game about how many hexadecimal multiplications you can answer in 60 seconds. Ready to learn your times tables in hex?

[From 0 to F]
Every question multiplies two single hex digits, 0 through F. The biggest answer is F × F = E1. Type your answer on the 0–F keypad.

[How many in 60 seconds?]
- A correct answer scores 10 points
- A wrong answer takes 5 points away and resets your streak
- Every 5 correct answers in a row adds 5 points per question, up to 25 points each

[High scores go to the ranking]
- Local Score: your top 50 past scores, best first
- Online Ranking: switch between the top 50 players worldwide and the players right around you
- Send your score or rank with SHARE

Free to play, with ads. Also available on iPhone.
